01 / The conference

Humanities as a force in public life.

“Empowering Humanities and Social Sciences” refers to the initiative to more fully incorporate human-centered disciplines such as sociology, history and education into nation-building and sustainability processes.

The conference foregrounds discourse analysis as a way of examining how language and narratives influence national identities, power structures and environmental policies, contributing to more inclusive and resilient societies.
